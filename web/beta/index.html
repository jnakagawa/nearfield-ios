NEARFIELD

a sound piece for many phones — beta

Your phone becomes one voice in a room-scale ensemble. When the app joins, it is given a pitch and a part in a tuning derived from the ensemble's own tone.

Move, and your voice opens. Come close to someone, and both your sounds bloom — beating slowly into tune with each other. Stand still long enough and you fade back into the field. The artwork happens in the air between speakers.

Every screen shows its own view of one shared interference field; a projection shows the whole.
