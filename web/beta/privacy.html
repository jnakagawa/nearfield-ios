NEARFIELD

Privacy policy · effective 2026-07-07

The short version

Nearfield has no accounts, no ads, no analytics SDKs, and no tracking. We don't know who you are and don't want to.

What the app senses, and where it goes

When the app joins an ensemble, it sends the coordination server a random device identifier (Apple's per-app "identifier for vendor") and your device's model name, and receives a musical part in return. While playing, it sends coarse ensemble telemetry about once per second: a wind/activity level, a bloom level, which participant number your phone is currently responding to, and a detune amount. That's the entire wire format.

Bluetooth is used only to sense which other participants are nearby; signal-strength readings are processed on your phone and never uploaded. Motion-sensor readings likewise never leave the device. The app records no audio and has no microphone access.

What we store

The server keeps the roster and latest telemetry of the current performance in memory to run the piece and its shared projection. Nothing is written to a database; restarting the server forgets everything.

What we share or sell

Nothing, with no one.
